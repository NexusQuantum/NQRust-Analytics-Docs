Every signed-in user has a profile page for managing their own account. Open it
from **Settings → Profile**.

## Your account

The profile page shows your:

- **Avatar** and **display name**
- **Email** address (used to sign in)
- **Role** — Admin, Editor, or Viewer (read-only here; only an administrator can
  change a user's role from [User Management](/docs/access-control/managing-access))

<Screenshot src="/images/docs/accounts/profile.png" caption="The Settings → Profile screen showing account details and the change-password section." />

## Update your display name

<Steps>

<Step>

### Edit the field

On **Settings → Profile**, change the **Display name** field.

</Step>

<Step>

### Save

Save the form. Your new display name is applied immediately.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Change your password

<Steps>

<Step>

### Enter your current and new password

In the **Change password** section, enter your **current password**, then your
**new password**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Save

Submit the form. Your password is updated immediately; use the new password the
next time you sign in.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Changing your password requires your current password. If you have forgotten
  it, contact an administrator, who can manage accounts from
  [User Management](/docs/access-control/managing-access).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  If you signed in through single sign-on, your password is managed by your
  identity provider. See [Single Sign-On](/docs/accounts/oauth).
</Callout>
