**NQRust Analytics** is an analytics platform that lets anyone query a
database in plain language. You ask a question, and the platform generates the
SQL, runs it, produces a chart, and explains the result — grounded in a semantic
model of your data so the answers remain consistent and governed.

It runs entirely on infrastructure you control.

## What you can do

<Cards>
  <Card title="Ask in natural language" href="/docs/get-started/installation">
    Turn business questions (in English or Indonesian) into correct, executable
    SQL — no SQL knowledge required.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Model your data" href="/docs/get-started/installation">
    Define models, relationships, calculated fields, and views with MDL so every
    answer uses the same trusted definitions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Visualize & share" href="/docs/get-started/installation">
    Auto-generate charts, build multiple dashboards, and share dashboards or chat
    threads with your team.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your own LLM" href="/docs/get-started/installation">
    Connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Azure, Ollama, and more through a single
    configuration layer.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Beyond the core

In addition to the analytical engine, NQRust Analytics provides capabilities for teams:

- **User accounts** — email/password sign-in, with optional OAuth.
- **Role-based access control** — Admin, Editor, and Viewer roles.
- **Multi-dashboard** — create and manage several dashboards per user.
- **Collaboration** — share dashboards and chat threads, star your favorites.
