NQRust Analytics is a self-hosted platform: there is no hosted organization,
billing, or seat management to configure. Instead, your single deployment hosts
everything you build.

## One deployment, many projects

A deployment hosts your projects. Each project pairs a data source with the
model and conversation history built on top of it. You create projects, switch
between them, and manage their settings directly in the Analytics UI. To create
or move between projects, see [Manage Projects](/docs/manage/project).

## User access

Rather than organization members and seats, NQRust Analytics uses **user
accounts** combined with **role-based access control (RBAC)**. Three roles are
available:

- **Admin** — full control over the deployment, projects, and settings.
- **Editor** — can build models and ask questions, but not manage the deployment.
- **Viewer** — read-only access to existing projects and results.

Accounts and roles have their own dedicated section, so this page only points you
in the right direction. To get your deployment running and create the first
account, see [Installation](/docs/get-started/installation).
